# Sprint 4.3.0 — Evaluation Independence, Scenario Diversity & Scientific Validation: Final Report

**Verdict: PARTIAL PASS.** Dataset independence, scenario diversity, evaluation infrastructure and statistical rigor improved
substantially and are verified. The main scientific hypothesis — that RIFT-FULL-V2 attributes root causes more accurately and
more usefully than meaningful alternatives — **was not established**. All results are *validated on synthetic ground-truth scenarios*
and are *intervention-consistent* only in the simulator's sense; they do not establish real-world causal attribution.

Companion documents: `docs/SPRINT4_3_0_DATASET_PROVENANCE_AUDIT.md`, `docs/EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2.md` (with Amendments 1-3),
`docs/SPRINT4_3_0_SCENARIO_DIVERSITY_REPORT.md`, `docs/SPRINT4_3_0_STATISTICAL_VALIDATION.md`, `docs/SPRINT4_3_0_FAILURE_TAXONOMY.md`.

## 1. Executive summary

* A new 360-scenario synthetic corpus (dev 180 / val 72 / held-out 108) was generated by a factorial, label-by-construction generator and
  verified independent on content, dynamics, topology, template and joint design cell: **0 cross-split leaks**, 0 QC failures, 0 group
  integrity violations, 0 near-duplicate pairs >= 0.90 across splits.
* The earlier held-out was found to be contaminated (all 15 legacy held-out scenarios have content twins in dev/val; the 83 legacy records
  contain only 25 distinct contents). It is retained as legacy and not used for any v2 claim.
* A pre-registered protocol (`EVALUATION_PROTOCOL_V2.md`), a freeze (37 files), a held-out guard and a one-shot held-out run were put in place and followed.
  The held-out set was opened **once**, after the freeze.
* On the held-out set (n = 108; 45 ATTRIBUTE, 63 ABSTAIN_REQUIRED) RIFT-FULL-V2 scored **P1 = 20/45 = 0.444**, **P2 = 60/108 = 0.556**,
  unsafe 37/108, coverage 57/108.
* **The trivial always-abstain policy scores P2 = 63/108 = 0.583. V2 does not outperform it** (post-hoc; difference -0.028, McNemar p = 0.76).
* V2 is **significantly better than six never-abstaining baselines on P2 and P3_safe** (Holm), but **not** on P1 (nominally below B6/B7: 20/45 vs 27/45),
  **not** better than B5-RIFT-OBS after Holm, and **not significantly better than legacy RIFT-FULL on P2 (p = 0.13)**.
* **H4 unsupported, H5 mixed, observational variants competitive.** An exploratory post-hoc variant (V2G) trades coverage and accuracy for safety and does not improve accuracy.
* The infrastructure and the negative findings are the durable results of the sprint.

## 2. Original objectives

1. Establish an evaluation independent of the previously inspected data: provenance audit, content-level independence verification, no seed-only independence.
2. Build a scenario corpus with broad, verifiable diversity (16 families, 11 structures, 11 temporal patterns, 10 workloads, 9 observability, 9 intervention, 11 negative kinds).
3. Fix the benchmark: identical observable windows and baselines for all methods, no ground-truth dependence in the harness, explicit denominators, abstention-aware metrics.
4. Pre-register hypotheses, metrics, statistics and thresholds, and freeze before touching the held-out set.
5. Test RIFT-FULL-V2 against the 8 non-oracle methods plus oracles and A1-A10 ablations, with clustered bootstrap, exact McNemar, permutation tests and Holm correction.
6. Report negative results with the same prominence as positive ones.

Objectives 1-4 and 6 were met. Objective 5 was met procedurally; the outcome on the substantive hypothesis was negative/mixed (section 7-10).

## 8. Statistical findings

12 of 24 confirmatory tests are significant after Holm correction (family = 8 non-oracle comparators x 3 metrics = 24 tests).

* Significant after Holm: P2 and P3_safe vs B0-EARLIEST, B0-HIGHEST, B1, B3, B6, B7 (Holm p <= 0.0028).
* **Not significant:** P1 against every comparator (V2 vs B6/B7 is -0.156, p = 0.14); P2 vs B5-RIFT-OBS (+0.167, Holm p = 0.115); P3_safe vs B5 (Holm p = 0.195);
  **P2 vs RIFT-FULL-LEGACY (+0.074, CI [-0.009, +0.159], p = 0.13, Holm 1.0)**; P3_safe vs legacy (McNemar p = 0.0169, Holm p = 0.186).
* Hypotheses: H1 partly met (6 of 8); **H2 not met nominally** (20/45 vs 27/45); H3 met (23/63 vs 57-63/63); **H4 unsupported**; **H5 mixed**.
* The large advantage on P2/P3 over never-abstaining baselines comes largely from abstention on a benchmark where 58 % of scenarios require abstention; the post-hoc always-abstain reference shows this.
* Full tables, effect sizes and CIs: `docs/SPRINT4_3_0_STATISTICAL_VALIDATION.md` and `artifacts/sprint4_3_0/results/held_out/statistics.json`.

## 13. Limitations

* **Synthetic only.** Telemetry, faults, interventions and recovery are simulator outputs. Intervention fidelity is a simulator assumption; there is no control arm, and non-stationary faults can produce spurious CID.
  Recovery time (30 s) is a model constant.
* **The benchmark favours abstention:** 58 % of held-out scenarios require it; WEAK_SIGNAL is concentrated in `high_baseline_variance`, `low_load` and `partial_degradation` by design (absolute amplitude vs varying sigma).
  P2 therefore rewards abstention and must be read with always-abstain, coverage and P1.
* **P1 is not better than the baselines;** the legacy-RIFT-FULL comparison is not significant; observational variants are competitive; H4 is unsupported; H5 is mixed.
* **Post-hoc analyses are visible and exploratory:** always-abstain reference, V2G (Amendment 3), strata, ablations, oracle comparisons. Only the 24 confirmatory tests are Holm-corrected.
* **Small samples.** 4 no-fault scenarios in val and held-out (FPR 2/4 is imprecise); 71 held-out templates; per-stratum n mostly < 15; unseen strata are small-n and descriptive.
* **Single deterministic draw;** no run-to-run variance.
* **CID grade** is `CANDIDATE` for every CID check recorded for RIFT-FULL-V2 (held-out 531/531; dev 555/555; val 314/314); interventional evidence rests on strict `validate_cid_for_r4` plus a Bonferroni-corrected permutation test.
* Legacy RIFT-FULL, RIFT-RANDOM and RIFT-ONE-SHOT use nominal interventions (no interventional evidence). B6 = B7 on this corpus.
* The adversarial runner label leak is documented, not fixed. The hidden-variable and high-variance cells are not a test of variance-aware detection (no high-variance ATTRIBUTE scenarios).
* Shared marginals (workload, observability, temporal, intervention): no distribution shift on those axes.
* **Claims not made:** real-world causal attribution, production readiness, that RIFT beats baselines in general, that interventional evidence helps.

## 14. Final verdict

**PARTIAL PASS.**

Met: independence verified on content with zero cross-split leaks; broad, documented diversity; a reproducible, frozen, one-shot held-out protocol; honest denominators; 64 new tests with the full suite green;
negative results preserved.

Not met: the central hypothesis that RIFT-FULL-V2 attribution is better than meaningful alternatives. V2 is statistically better than never-abstaining baselines on safety-aware metrics, but it is not better on attribution
accuracy (P1), not distinguishable from the trivial always-abstain policy on P2, not significantly better than the legacy RIFT-FULL, and its interventional component shows no demonstrated benefit over observational variants.

Pre-committed Rule 15.5 ("scenario or test counts alone never justify PASS") applies. PARTIAL PASS describes an improved evidence base, not an improved method.
